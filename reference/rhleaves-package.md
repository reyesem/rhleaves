# Measurements on leaves taken from the living walls on the Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology campus.

\[Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology\](https://www.rose-hulman.edu/)
maintains two "living walls" on campus. Each living wall is comprised of
a mosaic of six species of plants. Each of the plant has almond-shaped
leaves. A sample of leaves was taken in April 2025. This package
contains measurements taken on the leaves, an image of each leaf, and a
function for computing the area of a leaf from an image.

## Details

The dataset is called \`rhleaves\` and the function for computing the
dimensions of a leaf from its image is \`leaf_dimensions()\`.
